Questions.

What does GymKey do?+

It turns your iPhone into a training-only tool. Tap in, keep what helps your workout and leave the usual distractions outside the session.

Does it need charging?+

Never. Keep it on your keys and it is ready whenever you are.

Can I choose what stays available?+

Yes. Keep music, calls and the apps that genuinely help you train. Put everything else out of reach until you tap out.

Can I log workouts?+

Yes. Pick a saved workout after you tap in, then log sets, weight and rest without leaving your focused session.

Which phones work?+

GymKey is launching for compatible iPhones. Exact model and iOS requirements will be clear before pre-orders open.

When will it ship?+

We are finishing real-world testing first. You will see a clear delivery estimate before you are ever asked to pay.
