A deliberate ritual

Your phone changes jobs at the gym.

It keeps the useful parts—music, timers, the workout—and puts your chosen distractions behind Apple’s Screen Time shield.

  1. 01

    Tap in

    Open GymKey and scan the key at the top of your iPhone. Your tag is read, never overwritten.

  2. 02

    Train

    Your selected apps and sites are shielded. The day’s workout, rest timer and Spotify playlist stay ready.

  3. 03

    Tap out

    Finish the workout and scan the same GymKey. Training Mode clears locally and your normal phone returns.

The companion app

Fewer choices between sets.

The app is intentionally small. Set up what disappears, choose a playlist, see today’s plan, then get out of the interface and train.

  • 01 Apple’s private app and website picker
  • 02 Workout plan, set logging and rest timer
  • 03 Same-key finish with offline local state

The current public build is a product demo. Physical NFC and Screen Time enforcement are being validated in signed native builds before sale.

Built to live on your keys

A gym tool, not another device to charge.

The electronics are the simplest part: a passive NFC inlay inside a durable two-piece shell. The eyelet is molded plastic and the metal ring is isolated from the antenna.
